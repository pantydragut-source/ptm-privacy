Privacy Policy

PTM Shorts Publisher · Last updated: October 12, 2026

Who we are

PTM Shorts Publisher is a private, self-hosted publishing tool operated by the owner of the PTM Financ3 channels for his own accounts only.

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects his own YouTube, Google, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok account, the app receives an access token from that platform. It uses the token only to upload videos, set titles, descriptions and publishing times, and read basic channel or page information (name, profile picture, post status) needed to publish.

How data is stored and shared

Tokens and post data are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a self-hosted database. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other people is collected.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect any account at any time from the app or from the platform's security settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions), which revokes the token. To request deletion of any data, write to the contact address below.
